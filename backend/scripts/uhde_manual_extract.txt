1 INTRODUCTION 
Uhde AdministratorTM  belongs to the system of Uhde IntegratorTM. Therefor it links 
different data of the plant to achieve an integrated system to fulfill the long-term 
needs of the clients. 
The program comprises a relational database for the management of cell elements, 
operating and analytical data obtained in the Uhde Chlor-Alkali Electrolysis Plant. 
The program provides for complete documentation of the assembly, operating and 
analytical data and for the maintenance activities required for halfshells and 
membranes. 
The program was developed using Microsoft Access 97. 
Users should be familiar with personal computers and the operating systems 
Windows 95, Windows 98; Windows NT or Windows°XP

Electrolyzer and Position

  Grouping according to electrode coatings

provided for said groups. 
DOL (days on line) 
The DOL is calculated automatically and specifies the on-line period of the element 
in question in the specified position of the electrolyzer.

3.4 SEARCH FUNCTIONS 
The submenu “Search functions“ (Fig. 19) provides for simple generation of 
“Assembly data“ outputs arranged in groups according to various criteria. It is

   Example: Search for anode No. 
   Item:  A 874? 
   Input:  A 874* 
   Output: A 8741 to A 8749 
Pressing the “Update Display” button will then generate the search results.

All other buttons permit data extraction related to these inputs. These extractions 
are classified as follows: 
  Tables and diagrams showing standardized voltages of complete electrolyzer 
sets (Total Plant, Train, Electrolyzer, Groups or Elements) 
  Tables and diagrams showing standardized voltages of single elements 
  Tables and diagrams showing standardized voltages of groups

Fig.°21: Current efficiency

electrolyzers by clicking the respective buttons in this submenu.

from the operating data entered under the submenu “Standardized Voltage” and the 
analytical values entered under the submenu “Analyses”.

The current efficiency can only be calculated on the basis of current, voltage and

3.7 POWER CONSUMPTION 
The submenu “Power Consumption“ is accessed via the main menu (Fig. 1). 
Fig. 22: Power consumption 
Power consumption charts and tables are generated for individual electrolyzers by 
clicking the related buttons in this submenu.

energy consumption on the basis of the operating data contained in “Data Input 
Electrolyzers“ (refer to chapter 3.5 Standardized Voltage) and "Analysis" (Chapter 
3.10).

The power consumption can only be calculated on the basis of operating data 
(current) and analytical values measured on the same day.
