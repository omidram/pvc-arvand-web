# Narrative plan — Arvand Cell Management presentation

Audience: plant management and organization leadership at PVC Arvand (chlor-alkali / PVC). Persian, right-aligned. The deck is a presentation of the work itself: the designer’s UHDE Administration system was valuable, the years of scattered files lost that value, and the web program restores the designer’s data and finishes what Access only sketched.

Objective: make the scale and care of the work obvious, without inventing numbers.

## Arc
1. The plant, from the whole electrolyzer down to the three parts of one element.
2. How the designer (Uhde) managed those parts: UHDE Administration.
3. How deep that Access program already was.
4. What happened in between: Excel islands, island inspections, Parseh without real reporting.
5. The project: a web program founded on that Access, fed with the data the designer required.
6. What goes beyond Access: extra data, links, completed workflows for the element, the piece, purchase, coating, and the warehouse.

## Facts used (local plant database and the live main menu, 1405/07/27)
- Customer PVC Arvand, plant type NaCl, display version V 1.32.
- 2 trains, 24 electrolyzers A1–M2 (no letter I). Positions 1–168 per electrolyzer. 4,032 cell positions. 2,656 in service, 2,663 of 4,032 filled on the live board.
- Electrode area used by the cathodic-efficiency formula: 2.72 m². Design element count in that formula: 163.
- Element records 11,419. Anodes 5,371. Cathodes 5,427. Membranes 9,833. Electrode segregations 3,198. Voltage readings 1,493,351.
- Live board also shows 2,340 parts near end of life, 2,200 waiting to be sent for coating, occupancy 67.6%, parts-in-healthy-range 71.0%, 140 recoating items inside 90 days. L2 and M2 currently show 0 installed cells.
- 217 tables in the working database, including the Access archive.
- Program name on screen: Arvand Cell Management. Original product: Uhde Administrator / UHDE Administration (Microsoft Access, EAP).

## Visual system
Deep brine ink, membrane teal, copper, warm paper. Not generic corporate blue. Persian titles in B Nazanin, body in Vazir, right-aligned. Art plates are text-free atmosphere. Product proof is real screenshots of the running program.

## Image plan
One 16:9 text-free plate per slide, calm regions for cards. Screenshots: main menu, assembly workflow, monitoring cell room, cell arrangement, warehouse, element dashboard.

## Editability
Titles, numbers, comparison rows, workflow labels, and the record chart are PowerPoint text, tables, or a native chart. Screenshots and plates are images only.
